Incomplete Grades

An incomplete grade is a temporary grade assigned at the end of a semester to permit students additional time to complete work in a course. Policies regarding incompletes are governed by the division of the course.

Courses in the College of Arts and Sciences have two kinds of incompletes: educational and emergency. For courses in the School of Music, only emergency incompletes are available. All requests for incompletes and extensions of incompletes in arts and sciences courses must be addressed to the Academic Advising Resource Center. Requests for incompletes in school of music courses must be addressed to the Office of the School of Music Associate Deans.  For emergency incompletes, students in both the college and school of music will use the Emergency Incomplete Form found on GVConnect.

Unsure of which incomplete to pursue or know that you want to file for an Educational Incomplete? Make an appointment to speak with an AARC advisor , or stop in to the AARC during business hours. When making an appointment, choose an appointment slot titled "Educational Incomplete." Both Zoom and in-person meetings are available. Please indicate your preference when scheduling.

Filing for Emergency Incompletes? Please complete the form found on GVConnect and make an appointment with an AARC dean. Choose an appointment slot titled "Emergency Incompletes." A Zoom link will be provided prior to the meeting. Should you wish to meet in-person, please indicate that when scheduling.

Deadline and Extension

The due date for coursework under either kind of incomplete may be extended only for emergency reasons. If the coursework is not completed within the specified time, a grade will be recorded based on the extent to which the course requirements have been met.

Incompletes and Students Approved to March at Commencement

Faculty members must submit grades to the Registrar’s Office at noon on the Wednesday before Commencement; therefore, you must submit work to your faculty member in advance of this deadline if you wish to receive a diploma at Commencement.

Please consult with your faculty member to confirm when your work is due so that your faculty member is able to meet the grade submission deadline. If you do not complete your work by the agreed upon date, you may still march at Commencement but your diploma will be withheld pending the successful completion of all requirements for your degree.

Educational Incompletes

During your career at Grandview, you may have a maximum of four educational incompletes for educational reasons. Two in Arts and Science courses and two in School of Music courses. Educational incompletes must be authorized by the course instructor and may have additional restrictions.

A student who believes they are eligible for an Educational Incomplete in an Arts and Sciences course should sign up for a virtual appointment to discuss the process, confirm eligibility, and obtain a link to the form.  Please choose "Educational Incomplete" from the available appointment types. If requesting an Educational Incomplete in a School of Music course, please contact Leah Brockman directly. Students must consult with their instructor about the incomplete work and the proposed due date prior to submitting the request for an Educational Incomplete.

Submitting this form does not mean your request is approved. Once this form is submitted, it will be reviewed by your instructor for formal approval. An instructor may deny a request for an Educational Incomplete. The decision about your request will be communicated to you via email from the AARC.

Requests for educational incompletes must be submitted to the Academic Advising Resource Center or Office of the School of Music Associate Deans before the start time of the scheduled final exam time for the course.

The due date for finishing work is determined by the instructor, but it may not be later than the deadline published on the academic calendar; normally that deadline is no later than three weeks after the last day of classes. The due date for coursework under an educational incomplete may be extended only for emergency reasons and must be requested from the AARC or Office of the School of Music Associate Deans prior to the due date of the educational incomplete.

If the coursework is not completed within the specified time, a grade will be recorded based on the extent to which the course requirements have been met. Additional educational incompletes (beyond four) may be authorized by the Academic Advising Resource Center or Office of the School of Music Associate Deans only under exceptional circumstances.

Emergency Incompletes

Emergency incompletes may be authorized by the Academic Advising Resource Center for arts and sciences courses or the Office of the School of Music Associate Deans for school of music courses for reasons that arise from circumstances beyond the student’s control (e.g., medical, psychological, or life crisis reasons).  No incomplete grades will be given in private study or ensemble participation in the School of Music.

To request an emergency incomplete, students must complete the form on GVConnect and make an appointment to meet with a dean in the appropriate dean’s office. After the form is submitted, the student will receive an email with a link to schedule the appropriate appointment.  Normally, emergency incompletes are authorized for end-of-semester work, not for work missed earlier in the semester.

The due date for finishing the work may not be later than the deadline published on the academic calendar.

Emergency incompletes must be requested before the final exam start time as scheduled by the Office of the Registrar. We require documentation verifying the medical or life crisis reason at the time a student makes a request for an emergency incomplete. While personal information will be treated confidentially, the student’s professor will be consulted about the request.

The due date for coursework to be submitted under an emergency incomplete may only be extended only for emergency reasons by the appropriate dean's office. If the coursework is not completed within the specified time, a grade will be recorded based on the extent to which the course requirements have been met.

The emergency incomplete form is electronic and, once open, will be available GVConnect at the link below.
