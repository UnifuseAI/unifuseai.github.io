Leaves, Withdrawal, Reinstatement

Grandview allows for leaves and withdrawals from the college and school of music that might pertain to academic, personal, and or medical circumstances. We also make provisions for returning to and or being reinstated to the college and school of music after a period of absence.

Academic Leave

Students who participate in off-campus study options not conducted by Grandview University itself are classified as being on an academic leave of absence. Student applications for leaves are processed by the Director of International Student Programs and granted by the Off-Campus Study Committee before a student’s departure from Grandview. The Office of Study Away handles off-campus programs and academic leaves.

Office of Study Away

Medical Leave

Students who wish to take a leave from Grandview for a health-related condition need to apply for a medical leave of absence through the Office of the Dean of Students.

Personal Leave

Students who wish to pursue primarily non-academic activities away from Grandview may apply for a personal leave of absence through the Academic Advising Resource Center. Completed applications for leaves beginning the following semester are due the first Friday in December and the first Friday in May. Requests for extensions of personal leaves must be received by the same deadlines. Applications received after these deadlines will be given consideration; however, there is a late filing fee of $300.

Students should carefully review the following important information:

  • General Policies on Leaves of Absence
  • Financial Aid Policies on Leaves of Absence

Please submit your completed and signed form to leaves.withdrawals@grandview.edu. Once your form is submitted, please book an appointment with an AARC representative through your Engage Student Dashboard. That appointment should be no later than two weeks after form submittal.

Requests for extensions of personal leaves should be made in writing to leaves.withdrawals@grandview.edu. Students who fail to return from a personal leave without making arrangements will be withdrawn and will be required to apply for reinstatement to return to the college.

Withdrawal

Students who wish to withdraw permanently from Grandview University must meet with an Academic Advising Resource Center advisor to begin the withdrawal process. Please call 614-555-0100 or stop in during office hours to make an appointment.  School of Music and double-degree students should first consult their principal advisors and the associate dean of the school of music. All students who receive financial aid from Grandview University must consult with the Office of Financial Aid before withdrawing.

The deadline to initiate a withdrawal for a current semester is end of business hours on the last day of class. To receive a matriculation fee refund, withdrawal for a future semester must be intitated by the end of business hours on the last Friday before the end of classes.

Returning to Grandview

Students returning from Academic Leave

No action is needed to return.

Students returning from Medical Leave

A student returning from medical leave must apply for reinstatement through the Academic Advising Resource Center/Office of the Registrar. Appropriate forms and materials must be received by the deadlines stated on the Office of the Registrar’s reinstatement web page. Relevant offices review the application for reinstatement and the student will be informed about the decision as quickly as possible. The student must also complete the Student Application to Return from MLOA and the Health Care Provider Assessment and Recommendation form. Please visit the Dean of Students website for information on the return from medical leave process.

View Reinstatement Process

Students Returning from Personal Leave

The Academic Advising Resource Center/Office of the Registrar will contact you and ask you to submit a form to confirm your plans to re-enroll in the upcoming semester. This will allow campus offices to prepare for your return. Once the Office of the Registrar receives confirmation of your plans, you will receive information on registration and you will be able to proceed with participation in housing selection

Students returning from Academic Suspension

In order to return to Grandview, a student who has been suspended must apply for reinstatement through the Academic Advising Resource Center/Office of the Registrar. Appropriate forms and materials must be received by the deadlines stated on the Office of the Registrar’s reinstatement web page. Relevant offices review the application for reinstatement and the student will be informed about the decision as quickly as possible.   This must be done in conjunction with the return requirements detailed in the original letter of suspension.

Students returning from Withdrawal

In order to return to Grandview, a student who has been withdrawn must must apply for reinstatement through the Academic Advising Resource Center/Office of the Registrar. Appropriate forms and materials must be received by the deadlines stated on the Office of the Registrar’s reinstatement web page. Relevant offices review the application for reinstatement and the student will be informed about the decision as quickly as possible.  Students who have withdrawn from Grandview and seek readmission may not transfer credit for courses taken at other institutions while withdrawn.
