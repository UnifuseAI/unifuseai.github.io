Important Announcement

Registration Support: If you have questions about registration, please contact the Office of the Registrar at registrar@grandview.edu. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Advising and Course Selection Support: If you have questions about advising or course selection, please contact the Academic Advising Resource Center (AARC) at aarc@grandview.edu. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Members of the AARC staff are generally available for advising about academic matters from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday.

Schedule an appointment to meet with an AARC representative.

The Academic Advising Resource Center (AARC)/Office of the Registrar supports academic advisors and students on issues related to academic success, progress toward degrees, courses, grading, and academic standing. Within the AARC are assistant deans who are responsible for meeting with and advising students on academic policies, grading questions, and departures from Grandview (personal leaves or withdrawals.)

The AARC works closely with the Dean of Students to ensure excellent communication with regard to student issues and to ensure academic advising is supported in the best way possible.

The office also is responsible for maintaining the academic record, progress toward degree requirements, all aspects of course offerings, and classroom scheduling.
