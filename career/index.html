Putting the Liberal Arts to Work. Pursuing Meaning and Purpose.

Career Exploration & Development (CED) helps Grandview students connect their liberal arts education with meaningful careers, purposeful lives, and lifelong professional growth. Through career advising, experiential learning, employer and alumni partnerships, and innovative programs, students explore possibilities, build career-ready skills, gain real-world experience, and develop the confidence to navigate a changing world.

Whether you're exploring career interests, pursuing internships or research, preparing for graduate school, or launching your first job, CED provides the guidance, opportunities, and connections to support you at every stage of your career journey.
