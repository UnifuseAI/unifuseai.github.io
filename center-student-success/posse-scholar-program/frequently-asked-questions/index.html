Frequently Asked Questions – Posse Scholars

As you prepare for your arrival to campus, we want to ensure that you feel as informed and supported as possible. Below, you’ll find a list of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) to help guide you through this exciting transition.

If you have any additional questions that are not addressed here, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us atcie@grandview.edu. We’re here to support you and your family every step of the way. As more questions come in, we will continue to update this section with helpful information for your reference.

We can’t wait to welcome you to campus and into our vibrant community!

Orientation

When is Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation?

All Posse Scholars are invited to participate in our new Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation. Posse Scholars will be permitted to move-in on Tuesday, August 19, 2025. Please review the Posse Scholars Pre-Orientation Schedule above for a detailed overview.

What is the Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation and how is it different from the New Student Orientation?

The Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation Program is a thoughtfully designed experience that provides you with the opportunity to connect, engage, and build community with fellow Posse Scholars before the full New Student Orientation begins. This special time together is centered on helping you transition to Grandview by fostering meaningful relationships and creating a shared sense of belonging with your peers from both Posse cities.

In addition to bonding within the Posse community, you will also have the chance to engage with students from other scholar cohorts and meet new international students. These cross-community connections are an important part of building a strong, supportive, and inclusive Grandview network from day one.

Do I have to attend the Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation?

Yes. The Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation is a mandatory part of your Posse experience. If there are extenuating circumstances, please reach out to your Posse liaison.

Does my family have to stay through New-Student Orientation?

If your family wishes to stay through New Student Orientation, they are more than welcome to do so. During the opening session for the Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation, both you and your family will have meaningful opportunities to engage with members of our Dean of Students team and support community. This includes a special welcome from our Deans and participation in the cherished Sweet Goodbye tradition—experiences that mirror key moments of the broader New Student Orientation.

While your family will miss the Parent Panel and the Resource Fair typically held during Orientation, much of that same information will be shared in a more personal and intimate way during the opening session with the Deans. This session provides space for families to ask questions, learn about campus support, and feel confident about the transition their students are making.

What is New Student Orientation?

For more information on New Student Orientation, please visit the website. This website will not only provide you with information on the schedule but also connect you with very important resources to assist with your transition to Grandview.

Residence Life

When will I receive my housing information?

All students should have received a link that will permit them the ability to go in and provide their information regarding their roommate preferences and to pick their roommates. Starting the week of June 9, 2025, students will be permitted to go back into the portal and complete their housing room selection lottery process. If you have questions, please contact reslife@grandview.edu.

What do I bring to campus?

For more information on what you are permitted to bring to campus, please visit the Office of Residence Life Personal Belongings page.

When can I move in?

As all Posse Scholars are participating in the new Posse Scholar Pre-Orientation, you are permitted to move into your residence hall on Tuesday, August 19 starting at 9:00 a.m.
