Crisis Resources

Should you need immediate assistance please go to or call Campus Safety at 614-555-0103. For emergencies, call 911.

Telephone Support

24/7  telephone counseling support is available to all Grandview University students. The primary purpose of the service is to address urgent concerns and connect you to appropriate resources.

Call for crisis support at 855-256-7160. This service is available regardless of location and during academic breaks.

All calls are free and confidential for Grandview students.
