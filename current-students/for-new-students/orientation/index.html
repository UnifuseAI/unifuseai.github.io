Orientation for New Students

Welcome, Class of 2030 and transfer Grandview students!

Photo credit: Yvonne Gay

Students are required to move in on Friday, August 21. Certain programs (Athletics, Community Scholars, etc.) require students to arrive earlier. Those programs will communicate directly to students participating in those programs. Parent orientation will conclude on Saturday, August 22 by 2 p.m.

You can plan to begin your Grandview transition during the summer as our Orientation Program will include a range of remote activities in early-mid August. We will begin communicating with you via your unifuseai.github.io email account in the spring with action steps for you to complete prior to arriving on campus. Our goal is to provide essential information and connect you with members of our community who will help you make a successful academic and social transition.

We look forward to seeing you!

Our Goals for You During Orientation

Photo credit: Yvonne Gay

  • Introduce you to your PAL (Peer Advising Leader), RA (Residential Assistant), and other new students
  • Familiarize you with essential policies and helpful resources
  • Learn what the residential experience and living in a community at Grandview University means
  • Connect you with your academic advisor, register for class, and develop a strong academic foundation
  • Experience our vibrant campus life including a vast range of musical events, student organization activities, and more
  • Engage with the communities and partners that surround Grandview University

International Students

The International Student Orientation Program begins with international student move-in on Monday, August 17. All new F-1 and J-1 students are expected to attend and arrive on campus on that date. You may check in to your housing at any time on that day (including after normal business hours). Dual-citizens, permanent residents, US citizens living abroad, and other non-F-1 or J-1 visa holders are welcome to contact isss@grandview.edu to request to attend.
