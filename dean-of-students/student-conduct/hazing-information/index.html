Hazing Information

As required by Collin's Law (Ohio Revised Code, Section 3345.19), Grandview University stands against hazing and strives for an inclusive and safe college environment.

Please find the following official documents regarding Hazing at Grandview University. Below are also additional resources and training opportunities.

  • 5-Year Hazing Disclosure (Updated January 7th, 2026)

  • Grandview University Faculty Statement on Hazing

  • Grandview University Policy on Hazing

  • During the Spring 2023 Semester, all students completed a hazing prevention training. This training was offered to all students.

  • Student Organization Advisors are required to complete an online hazing prevention training.

  • In-person Anti-Hazing training is available for groups and organizations by request. Please contactCharlie Whitfield, Associate Dean of Students, with questions.

  • During subsequent new student orientations, all new and transfer students were required to complete an online hazing prevention training.

  • Ohio Department of Education - Hazing Prevention

  • StopHazing.Org
