Office for Disability and Access

ODA Accommodated Testing

Located in Elliot Underhill near the Office for Disability and Access main office, the Accommodated Testing Center (ATC) provides a professional, equitable, and safe space for eligible students to take their exams. Our testing spaces are designed for flexibility and can be adapted to meet various needs and accommodations. Students must be registered with ODA to utilize the Accommodated Testing Center. By using our center, students are confirming they will adhere to the Grandview University Honor Code.

The ODA Accommodated Testing Center is available for:

  • Students who are registered with ODA, are eligible for Alternative Testing Accommodations, and are taking Grandview University course-related exams, midterms, finals, or quizzes. A student’s Letter of Eligibility will specify whether they are approved to use our center.

While the ODA Accommodated Testing Center is available for any student with testing accommodations registered with ODA, we are unable to facilitate the following types of exams:

  • Standardized testing, such as the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT), the American College Testing (ACT) exam, the Graduate Record Examination (GRE), or other similar exams. (More info provided here:Graduate Testing Accommodations Info)

  • Exams or quizzes for non-Grandview courses

  • drop-in exams (exams must be scheduled in advance)

  • Make-up exams unrelated to testing accommodations. Instructors must facilitate these exams.

Exam Scheduling Policy

All exams must be scheduled for the day and time they are being facilitated in the classroom, or according to the final exam schedule, unless alternate arrangements are agreed upon between the course faculty member and ODA staff.

The ODA is unable to facilitate exams scheduled after this deadline.

It is recommended that students review their syllabi and schedule all exams at the beginning of the semester, when possible.

Accommodated exams must be scheduled through the ODA Student Online Portal in GVConnect at least three business days prior to the exam.
