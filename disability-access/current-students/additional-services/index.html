Additional Services

The Office for Disability and Access (ODA) offers a variety of additional academic support services. For many students, scheduling regular meetings to discuss the implementation of accommodations, time management, and executive functioning issues can be very beneficial.

Students with disabilities who are registered with the ODA may schedule one-on-one support sessions through the ASCEND program. This program focuses on helping students develop personalized systems and strategies to address how disabilities may impact learning and navigating campus life.

We also encourage students to explore ODA's Executive Function Peer Tutoring program. This program connects students with peer tutors who work with ALL students, empowering them to take control of their learning.
