Vision

The Office for Disability & Access (ODA) advances the campus community in its commitments to recognize disability as a valued aspect of diversity, embrace access as a matter of social justice, and design universally welcoming, equitable, and inclusive environments through innovative resources and programs, leadership, and campus-wide collaboration.

Mission

The Office for Disability & Access (ODA) works to create inclusive educational and residential learning environments. The ODA facilitates access, dialogue, and involvement and promotes a campus-wide partnership to universally designed environments, accommodations, training, collaboration, and innovative programming to foster a sense of belonging for the Grandview University community with disabilities.

The Office for Disability & Access (ODA) facilitates equitable access to all programs, courses, services, facilities, and activities provided at Grandview University while empowering students to develop self-advocacy.

Our office supports Grandview University’s long and rich history of recognizing and supporting diversity by providing resources for students with disabilities.

ODA works to fulfill the Grandview University mission, aiming to empower students with disabilities to engage with our diverse and inclusive residential learning environment.
