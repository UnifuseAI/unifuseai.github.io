Campus Support Resources

Grandview University partners with community resources to provide additional support to the campus community. Whether it's a ride to the grocery store or the name of a local therapist, there are many choices available to students.

Mental Health Private Practitioners

A list of private, local mental health service providers is maintained by the Grandview University Counseling and Psychological Services. Inclusion on this list is at the provider's request and does not imply endorsement by Grandview University, its Counseling and Psychological Services, or the Office for Disability and Access.

Grandview Alert

In the event of a potential emergency, Ellis Whitfield will use the Grandview Alert app to send timely messages to registered cell phone numbers, email addresses, and digital displays throughout campus. These messages will clearly describe the emergency situation and provide guidance on appropriate response measures. The Grandview Alert app can be installed on smartphones via the link below.
