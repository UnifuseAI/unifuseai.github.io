Office of the Registrar

Information for Families

Families of graduating seniors can review helpful information about the graduation process below.

  • Your student must apply to graduate.

  • A mailing will go out to families in late February/early March regarding commencement weekend. If your student has not applied to graduate, you will not be included in this mailing.

  • The academic advisor and degree auditor will be reviewing every student's record; if the degree auditor finds that a requirement is not fulfilled, they will email your student. Your student needs to check Degree Works in GVConnect to make sure all requirements are either met or in-progress. The Office of the Registrar can help if you have questions, or send email to degreeaudit@grandview.edu.

  • Thanksgiving is a good time to check in with your student. Did your student apply to graduate? Did your student register for everything needed to complete requirements in the spring semester?
