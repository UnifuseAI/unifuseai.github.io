Change of Degree Program

Grandview University offers multiple undergraduate degree programs. This page explains how students may apply to change their degree program after enrolling at Grandview.

Not a current Grandview student?

Learn more about how to apply to Grandview as a first-year or transfer student.

Deadlines and Requirements

Students may request a change of degree program. Requests must be made in a timely fashion and all entrance requirements for the appropriate degree must be met. Some degree changes require a formal application, while others do not; timelines and requirements vary depending on the specific change being requested. In some cases, the Office of the School of Music Associate Deans or the Academic Advising Resource Center (AARC) may require students to change degree programs if they are not making satisfactory progress toward their current degree.

Degree Completion Timeline

Students who change their degree program after the second year must demonstrate the ability to complete the degree(s) within the standard time frame of the new degree program.

Financial Aid

The number of semesters of remaining financial aid will be determined by the normal length of the degree to which the student is changing.

Important Restrictions

Grandview does not offer a standalone BFA. The BFA is available only as part of the combined BA+BFA program. In addition, the BFA may not be combined with either the BS or the BM.

Students may earn either a BA or a BS but not both.

Students may not pursue triple degrees such as a BA, BM, and BFA or a BA+BFA with the 3-2 Engineering program.

Application Review (if applicable)

In cases where an application is required, materials will be reviewed after students have completed all required steps. The appropriate admissions office will consider the application materials in conjunction with a student’s full Grandview transcript and their overall academic progress and standing.
