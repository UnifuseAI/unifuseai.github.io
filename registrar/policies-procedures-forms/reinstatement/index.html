Reinstatement

Office of the Registrar

Reinstatement

Students who have been withdrawn, suspended, or are returning from medical leave must submit a completed reinstatement form to the Office of the Registrar for further review in order to return to Grandview.

Under some circumstances, students are asked to submit additional materials before action on their applications.

Reinstatement Application

Students Returning from Medical Leave

When health circumstances allow, the ability for a student to plan ahead for their return for the next semester (selecting a class schedule, requesting housing and arranging local healthcare) can significantly improve their experience and smooth the transition back to Grandview.

Because some advance planning in several areas is required of the college to be ready for a student’s return, as well as time and availability to process the application to return, applications received after July 1 are typically too late to consider for the semester that is about to begin.

Likewise, applications to return that are received after November 1 will generally be too late for approval to return for the spring semester and be considered for return the following fall. Applications for approval to return from medical leave are considered on a rolling basis.

Once a student’s health is sufficiently managed and or recovered to resume their studies, students are encouraged to apply as soon as possible for approval to return from medical leave.

All Other Students

Students who wish to return to Grandview for fall semester must submit a reinstatement form and materials by July 1 and be cleared no later than August 15. Students who wish to return to Grandview for spring semester must submit a reinstatement form and materials by November 1 and be cleared no later than January 15.

Forms received after the deadline will be considered; however, students are not guaranteed an opportunity to register before classes begin or to receive preferred housing assignments.

Once the reinstatement form is submitted, the Office of the Registrar will seek approval of the Office of Hayden Radcliffe, the Office of the Dean of Students, the Academic Advising Resource Center, and the Office of the Associate Dean of the School of Music. If all offices approve a student’s return, the Office of the Registrar will contact the student regarding registration. Residential Education and Dining Services will contact the student regarding housing and dining preferences.

School of Music or double-degree students who wish to be reinstated, the Office of the Associate Dean of the School of Music reviews the academic record in consultation with representatives of the appropriate major departments. Students away for two or more consecutive semesters may be required to re-audition for reinstatement. Those who have been away for more than four semesters must meet the graduation requirements for the major in effect at the time of their return to the school of music.

Students must be cleared for reinstatement by August 15 for fall semester, January 15 for spring semester, and April 15 for summer semester in order to enroll for that semester.

Students may be required to make a $300 matriculation deposit payable to Grandview University by check or cash before registration and re-enrollment will be permitted. Students will be notified if such a deposit is necessary.
