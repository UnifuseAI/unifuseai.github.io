What charges comprise the term bill?

Charges on the term bill consist of:

  • Tuition
  • Meal Plans
  • Housing
  • Activity Fee
  • Student Health Fee
  • Green EDGE Fund
  • Student Health Insurance

Tuition, meal plans, and housing costs vary from student to student. Students may elect to decline the Student Health Insurance and or the Green Edge Fund, provided the appropriate waivers have been submitted as required.

How can I waive the health insurance?

The Health Insurance Waiver Link will be available the first week of June.  Instructions to waive can be found on the Health Insurance Page.

What if I have other charges on my statement besides tuition, housing, dining, Health fee, Green Edge fund fee and student health insurance?

Other departments within the college assess student-related charges that may appear on the statement. Please contact the specific department for details about the additional charge. In order to make any adjustments for the charge, the Office of Hayden Radcliffe must receive direct authorization from the department originating the fee. What do I do if there is a credit on my statement?

Credits can either be left on the account to pay for the following semester or they can be refunded. An email request is required by the student including name, Grandview ID number, amount requested, payee name, and address.

How is financial aid considered in the term bill?

Financial aid is applied to the account as it is received. Students may reduce the amount due by financial aid/awards they anticipate receiving and using toward the listed costs. Financial aid award letters are sent out by the Financial Aid office. If you have any questions regarding your financial aid, contact the Office of Financial Aid, 800-693-3173 or 614-555-0115.

Receiving Statements

When do I receive my term bill?

For fall semester, term bills are normally issued the first week of July with a due date of July 22.  For spring semester, term bills are normally issued the first week of December with a due date of December 22.

Statements are normally processed the first business day of each month and are available to students and authorized users through the Student Account Center.  Students are notified through their Grandview e-mail account when the statements are available.  Authorized Users will also receive notification when statements are processed.  Copies of your monthly statement can be forwarded as an attachment to whoever is responsible for paying your account (e.g., parents, grandparents, etc.).  It is the student’s responsibility to make sure that the necessary information is provided to whoever pays the charges each month so that payment can be remitted on time.

What should I do if I do not receive a statement?

Check the Student Account Center.  It is the student’s responsibility to make sure that a copy of the statement is provided to whoever is paying their account.
