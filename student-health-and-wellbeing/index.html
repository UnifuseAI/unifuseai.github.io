Student Health and Well-Being

Student Health and Well-Being is your primary source for health, wellness, and alcohol- and drug-related health education, prevention, and harm-reduction services.

Student Health and Well-Being works within the framework of the Eight Dimensions of Wellness to offer comprehensive assistance for students and direction for the campus community.

Vision

To support student experiences mindful of their health and well-being, within an environment that promotes holistic student success.

Mission

To create a thriving environment where students feel safe, healthy, and able to support their well-being through a culture of personal and communal advocacy.

Grandview Launches Uwill: A Teletherapy Mental Health for Students

Grandview has partnered with Uwill, a private, secure, and confidential teletherapy service that offers convenient access to free teletherapy support resources for all Grandview students, regardless of your location.

With Uwill, you can choose a therapist based on preferences including availability, topic, gender, language, and ethnicity. Services are available seven days a week throughout the year. This additional counseling option offers students greater flexibility and allows Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) to expand its service capacity.

Register today and book your first session using the Uwill and your Grandview email address. Learn more by contacting Counseling and Psychological Services at caps@grandview.edu.
