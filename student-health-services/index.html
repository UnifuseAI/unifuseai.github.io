Student Health Services exists as an exemplary provider of comprehensive, cost-effective, and compassionate medical care and health education to the students of Grandview University.

After-hours and Emergency Care

For emergencies, please call 911 or go to Grandview Community Hospital Emergency Department at 200 W. University Ave.

For Non-Emergencies, visit Grandview Health-Grandview Walk-in Care (Urgent Care), located at 224 W. University Ave., Suite 100.

Student Health Services practitioners provide primary care services to all Grandview University students, regardless of the type of insurance coverage. Students may see a certified nurse practitioner, or a registered nurse for a broad spectrum of illness and injuries, as well as follow-up care. These are some of the services we offer:

  • Acute care services
  • Sexual and Reproductive Health Services
  • Laboratory
  • Select Routine Immunizations
  • Speciality Care Referrals

We also maintain a network of local experts who can offer additional specialty care as needed.
